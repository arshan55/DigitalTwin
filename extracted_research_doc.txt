Novelty and Research Contribution
Existing studies mainly focus on individual pollutant estimation, AQI prediction, or climate-variable forecasting using satellite, ground, and meteorological data. The novelty of Model 1 is the integration of multi-pollutant surface AQI estimation with HCHO hotspot detection and fire-based pollution-source analysis in a unified India-wide framework.
The novelty of Model 2 is the development of an AI-powered Climate Digital Twin of India that integrates climate variables with Model 1 air-quality outputs to provide forecasting, climate-risk assessment, and what-if scenario simulation. Together, both models create an integrated system for monitoring, predicting, and assessing India's air-quality and climate conditions.
